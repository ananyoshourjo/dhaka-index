# Code of conduct

We want Dhaka Index to be a welcoming, practical community for job seekers,
self-hosters, designers, and developers.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Assume good faith while addressing technical evidence directly.
- Protect private information and disclose security problems privately.
- Respect publishers, job seekers, and removal requests.
- Give credit and accept correction.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized attention, deliberate
misinformation, credential sharing, privacy violations, and attempts to bypass
source access controls are not accepted.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close contributions, or restrict
participation when necessary to protect the community or project.

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers. Reports will be
reviewed confidentially and as promptly as practical.
